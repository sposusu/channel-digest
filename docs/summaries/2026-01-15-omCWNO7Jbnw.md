# Linus Media Group 財務大公開：錢都花去哪了？

## 重點摘要
Linus Tech Tips (LMG) 的兩大支出是 LTTStore.com 的庫存（佔 26%）和人事成本（佔 25%）。Linus 詳細解釋了公司的各項開銷，從薪資結構到營運細節，並說明約 15% 的淨利用於公司再投資、緊急預備金以及股東分紅，藉此強調公司對財務透明、員工福祉和獨立經營的承諾。

## 故事大綱
- **開場**：繼上次討論 LMG 如何賺錢後，Linus 承諾這次將完全透明地公開 LMG 如何花錢，並強調公司的決策始終以觀眾為核心。
- **中段**：Linus 揭露了詳細的支出結構。最大的兩項是**庫存採購 (26%)** 和**人事薪酬 (25%)**，兩者合計超過總支出的 50%。其次是**產品配送 (10%)**，包含倉儲與運輸費用。其他費用包括銷售平台手續費 (3%)、租金 (2%)、軟體授權 (1.5%)、研發 (1%)，以及包含公司活動、保險、水電等在內的龐雜「間接成本」。他深入探討了人事細節，公開 2025 年員工薪資中位數約為 8 萬加幣，並透露 CEO 與員工薪資中位數的比例介於 5:1 到 10:1 之間。
- **結尾**：最後約 15% 是公司淨利。Linus 解釋這筆錢並非直接進入他的口袋，而是用於三大目的：**再投資**（如拍攝新系列內容）、維持**營運現金流與緊急預備金**（這讓公司在經歷爭議事件時也從未裁員），以及支付**股東分紅**。他坦誠地討論自己作為創辦人與主要創作者的報酬，認為這是承擔創業風險後應得的回報，並強調 LMG 樂於成為培養產業人才的搖籃。

## 關鍵見解
1.  **人事與庫存為最大成本**：超過 50% 的支出用於員工薪酬和 LTTStore.com 的產品庫存，反映了 LMG 作為媒體與電商公司的雙重屬性。
2.  **以透明度建立信任**：影片選擇公開極度敏感的財務資訊，旨在向觀眾與團隊展示誠信，建立互信關係。
3.  **員工優先的薪酬策略**：LMG 致力於提供高於地區水平的薪資，並將內部最低工資與當地「生活工資」掛鉤，展現了其作為雇主的社會責任。
4.  **為創業風險辯護**：Linus 將自己可觀的收入定位為在資本主義體系中，承擔巨大創業風險與辛勤工作後所獲得的合理回報。
5.  **利潤是穩定與成長的基石**：公司淨利被視為策略性資金，用以確保內容創新、抵禦營運風險（避免裁員），並維持公司獨立性，不受外部投資者干預。

## 精彩時刻

# LMG's Finances Revealed: Where Does the Money Go?

## TL;DR
Linus Media Group's (LMG) two biggest expenses are inventory for LTTStore.com (26%) and payroll (25%). Linus breaks down all company spending, from salary structures to operational costs, and explains that the ~15% net profit is used for reinvestment, a rainy-day fund, and shareholder dividends, underscoring a commitment to financial transparency, employee welfare, and independent operation.

## Story Flow
- **Beginning**: Following up on a video about how LMG makes money, Linus promises to be fully transparent about how LMG spends its money, emphasizing that all decisions are guided by serving the audience.
- **Middle**: Linus reveals the detailed expense structure. The two largest categories are **Inventory Purchases (26%)** and **People/Payroll (25%)**, which together account for over 50% of total spending. This is followed by **Product Fulfillment (10%)**, covering warehousing and shipping. Other costs include selling fees (3%), rent (2%), software (1.5%), R&D (1%), and a broad "overhead" category with company events, insurance, and utilities. He dives deep into the payroll details, revealing the 2025 median salary is around $80,000 CAD and the CEO-to-median employee pay ratio is between 5:1 and 10:1.
- **End**: The final ~15% is the company's net profit. Linus explains this isn't just his personal fun money; it serves three main purposes: **reinvestment** in new projects, maintaining **operating cash flow and a rainy-day fund** (which allowed them to avoid layoffs during controversies), and paying **shareholder dividends**. He candidly discusses his own compensation as a founder and creator, framing it as the reward for entrepreneurial risk, and ends by highlighting LMG's pride in being a pipeline for industry talent.

## Key Insights
1.  **People & Inventory are the Biggest Costs**: Over 50% of expenses go to employee payroll and product inventory for LTTStore.com, reflecting LMG's dual nature as both a media and a product company.
2.  **Transparency Builds Trust**: The video is a deliberate act of sharing highly sensitive financial information to demonstrate integrity and build trust with the audience and team.
3.  **Employee-First Compensation Strategy**: LMG aims to provide above-average salaries for its region and pegs its internal minimum wage to the local "living wage," showcasing its commitment as a responsible employer.
4.  **A Defense of Entrepreneurial Reward**: Linus frames his significant personal income as the justified outcome of taking immense risks and working hard within a capitalist system.
5.  **Profit is the Foundation of Stability & Growth**: Net profit is treated as a strategic fund to ensure content innovation, absorb operational shocks (avoiding layoffs), and maintain independence from outside investors.

## Notable Moments
- The reveal that inventory costs for LTTStore account for a quarter of the company's entire expenses.
- The explanation that the company pays "rent" on buildings it owns for tax and liability purposes.
- The CEO-to-median employee pay ratio is disclosed to be between 5:1 and 10:1, which he calls "reasonable."
- Linus directly addresses his team about compensation, profit, and his personal earnings in a frank discussion.
- LMG's policy of encouraging and even sponsoring former employees' ventures, positioning the company as a "talent pipeline."
