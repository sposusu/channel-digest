# 為創投家（VC）打造 AI 助理

## 重點摘要
LangChain 的 Brace 在舊金山街頭，向多位風險投資家（VC）展示了一款名為「Agent Builder」的無程式碼工具。該工具讓使用者僅需用簡單的自然語言描述，就能立即建立功能強大的 AI 助理，以解決如管理電子郵件、追蹤產業動態和經營社群媒體等真實的工作挑戰。

## 故事大綱
- **開場**：影片開始，LangChain 的 Brace 在街頭隨機採訪穿著 T-shirt 和牛仔褲的風險投資家，詢問他們工作中最大的挑戰是什麼。
- **中段**：創投家們普遍提到，痛點在於處理堆積如山的電子郵件、跟上飛速發展的 AI 新知，甚至有人希望能成為 LinkedIn 上的意見領袖。Brace 順勢提議，可以為他們打造一個專屬的 AI 助理來解決這些問題。當他們表示自己「不會寫程式」時，Brace 保證這完全不成問題。
- **結尾**：創投家們被帶到電腦前，使用 Agent Builder 工具。他們僅用一句話描述自己的需求（例如：「幫我成為 LinkedIn 名人」、「自動幫我發郵件以節省時間」），就成功建立了自己的 AI 助理。他們對成果感到極度驚訝與興奮，並開玩笑說要回去跟同事炫耀這「花了他兩週」才完成的專案。

## 關鍵見解
1.  **AI 開發的普及化**：建立 AI 助理不再是開發者的專利，無程式碼工具讓任何有想法的人都能快速實現。
2.  **自然語言即是指令**：解決複雜商業問題（如郵件管理、社群經營）的門檻，已從撰寫程式碼降低到僅需清晰地描述需求。
3.  **快速實現價值**：LangChain 的 Agent Builder 工具展示了 AI 應用的敏捷性，能將一個想法在幾秒鐘內轉化為可用的工具。
4.  **專業人士的共同痛點**：即便是頂尖的創投家，也面臨著資訊過載和時間管理等普遍性難題，而這些正是 AI 能發揮巨大價值的地方。

## 精彩時刻
- 創投家們的真實困擾：「絕對是 email」、「要跟上所有 AI 的新東西」、「我想成為一個 LinkedIn 網紅」。
- 對於自己動手做的第一反應：「我不會寫程式。」
- 見識到工具後的驚嘆：「就這樣？一個提示詞就搞定？」、「太不可思議了。」
- 建立成功後的幽默感：「我要跟同事說這花了我兩個禮拜才做出來。」

# Building AI Agents for VCs on the Street

## TL;DR
Brace from LangChain hits the streets of San Francisco to show Venture Capitalists how its no-code "Agent Builder" tool works. The VCs use simple, one-sentence prompts to instantly create powerful AI agents that solve their real-world job frustrations, like managing email, tracking industry news, and building a social media presence.

## Story Flow
- **Beginning**: The video opens with Brace from LangChain interviewing casually-dressed VCs on the street, asking them about the most challenging parts of their job.
- **Middle**: The VCs' problems are relatable: overwhelming email inboxes, the struggle to keep up with AI trends, and a desire to become a LinkedIn influencer. Brace proposes building a custom AI agent to solve these issues, assuring them that "you don't need to know how to code."
- **End**: The VCs are brought to the Agent Builder tool and, with single prompts like "Help me become famous on LinkedIn" and "send emails that free up my time automatically," they successfully create their agents. They are left amazed and thrilled by the tool's simplicity and power, joking that they'll take credit for the "two weeks" of work.

## Key Insights
1.  **The Democratization of AI Development**: Building AI agents is no longer exclusive to developers. No-code tools are making it accessible to anyone with an idea.
2.  **Natural Language is the New Command Line**: The barrier to solving complex business problems (like email management or content creation) has been lowered from writing code to simply describing what you need.
3.  **From Idea to MVP in Seconds**: The Agent Builder demonstrates the agility of modern AI applications, allowing for the rapid creation and deployment of a functional tool.
4.  **The Common Pains of Professionals**: Even high-level professionals like VCs face universal challenges with information overload and time management, highlighting prime opportunities for AI intervention.

## Notable Moments
- **The VCs' honest problems**: "Definitely email," "Keeping up with all the AI stuff going on," "I think I want to become a LinkedIn influencer."
- **The immediate objection to building it themselves**: "I can't code."
- **The moment of pure astonishment**: "That's it. One prompt." followed by "Unbelievable."
- **The humorous takeaway**: "I'm going to tell everyone at work this took me two weeks."
