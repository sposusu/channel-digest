# WAN Show：Fallon脫口秀技術失誤、NVIDIA GPU風雲

## 重點摘要
Linus在「今夜秀」上展示聯想可捲動螢幕筆電時，因設備過熱導致展示失敗，讓他感到極度尷尬。本集深入探討了這次直播事故的幕後故事、對電視製作專業性的讚嘆，以及關於NVIDIA GPU停產、價格上漲和可能重新生產舊款型號的最新消息。

## 故事大綱
- **開場**：Linus和Luke首先聊到Linus最近登上「今夜秀」的經歷。Linus難掩興奮，但隨即透露他準備的重點展示——聯想的可捲動螢幕筆電——在直播中完全失靈。
- **中段**：Linus詳細描述了當時的窘境。他懷疑筆電在後台背包裡持續運行導致過熱，觸發了馬達的過熱保護。儘管他感到極度尷尬，但主持人Jimmy Fallon和團隊的專業反應成功化解了尷尬場面。Luke也分享了他在後台的緊張視角。他們接著讚揚了「今夜秀」製作團隊的專業、高效和對細節的重視。
- **結尾**：話題轉向科技新聞，討論了NVIDIA 5070 Ti和5060 Ti 16G版因記憶體限制而面臨停產和價格飆升的傳聞，儘管華碩後來澄清。同時，他們也提到了NVIDIA CEO黃仁勳考慮重新生產舊款GPU以應對市場短缺和高價問題的可能性。

## 關鍵見解
1.  **現場演示的風險極高**：即使經過演練，高壓環境和不可預見的技術問題（如過熱）也可能導致意外失敗。這次事件提醒我們，原型產品在直播中的表現充滿不確定性。
2.  **專業主義化解危機**：Jimmy Fallon和其團隊展現了頂級的專業素養，他們迅速且幽默地應對了技術故障，將潛在的災難轉化為一個有趣的電視片段，凸顯了經驗豐富的製作團隊的重要性。
3.  **GPU市場的波動性**：供應鏈問題（特別是記憶體）能迅速影響高階GPU的供貨和價格。NVIDIA停產高VRAM型號、合作夥伴的生產決策以及考慮重啟舊產線，都反映了市場的複雜和不穩定。
4.  **傳統媒體的製作價值**：「今夜秀」的幕後製作流程讓Linus和Luke大開眼界，其組織的嚴密性、時程的緊湊以及對所有平台內容（包括YouTube）的投入，展現了傳統媒體依然保有的高度專業水準。
5.  **透明與溝通的重要性**：Linus在節目中坦誠分享了他的失敗經歷和感受，這種透明度讓觀眾更能理解幕後發生的真實情況，拉近了與社群的距離。

## 精彩時刻
- Linus形容自己當時的狀態是「完全像隻被車頭燈照到的小鹿，我嚇壞了。」
- 在展示失敗後，Linus一心只想趕快下台解決問題，他直接「預定了它（booked it）」——快速衝下舞台，完全忽略了與主持人互動的慣例，這一幕讓他事後回看時也大笑不止。
- 對於GPU復活的議題，Linus開玩笑地說：「只要我們能弄到足夠的晶圓，那我其實很喜歡這個主意，因為你可以因為很多理由討厭NVIDIA，但你不能討厭他們對產品的長期支援。」
- 談到特斯拉將FSD改為訂閱制，Linus的同事在筆記中吐槽：「FSD甚至還沒兌現承諾，他（馬斯克）就已經拿走了直接購買的選項。」

# WAN Show: The Fallon Demo Fiasco & NVIDIA GPU Drama

## TL;DR
Linus recounts his mortifying experience on The Tonight Show when the Lenovo rollable laptop demo failed, likely due to overheating. The episode provides a deep dive into the behind-the-scenes story of the on-air mishap, admiration for the show's professionalism, and the latest news on NVIDIA GPU discontinuations, price hikes, and the potential resurrection of older models.

## Story Flow
- **Beginning:** Linus and Luke kick off by discussing Linus's recent appearance on The Tonight Show. Linus expresses his excitement but quickly reveals that his headline demo—the Lenovo rollable screen laptop—completely failed to work during the live segment.
- **Middle:** Linus details the cringe-worthy moment. He suspects the laptop, left running in his backpack backstage, overheated and triggered a thermal failsafe in the motors. Despite his deep embarrassment, he praises host Jimmy Fallon and the crew for their professional handling of the situation, which saved the segment. Luke shares his tense perspective from backstage. They follow up with praise for The Tonight Show's production team, highlighting their efficiency, professionalism, and attention to detail.
- **End:** The conversation shifts to tech news, covering rumors that the NVIDIA 5070 Ti and 5060 Ti 16G are being discontinued due to memory constraints, leading to price spikes (though ASUS later clarified). They also discuss NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang considering the possibility of resurrecting older GPUs to address shortages and high prices.

## Key Insights
1.  **The High-Stakes Risk of Live Demos:** Even with practice, unforeseen technical issues (like overheating) in a high-pressure environment can lead to failure. The incident is a stark reminder of the unpredictability of prototype hardware in a live setting.
2.  **Professionalism Turns a Crisis into Content:** Jimmy Fallon and his team showcased top-tier professionalism by reacting quickly and humorously to the technical glitch. They turned a potential disaster into an entertaining TV moment, highlighting the value of an experienced production crew.
3.  **The Volatility of the GPU Market:** Supply chain issues, especially with VRAM, can rapidly impact the availability and pricing of high-end GPUs. NVIDIA's rumored move away from high-VRAM models, partners' production decisions, and the consideration of restarting old lines reflect a complex and unstable market.
4.  **The Production Value of Traditional Media:** Linus and Luke were deeply impressed by the behind-the-scenes operation of The Tonight Show. The tight organization, fast-paced schedule, and genuine care for content on all platforms (including YouTube) demonstrated a level of professionalism that traditional media still commands.
5.  **The Power of Transparency:** Linus’s candid retelling of his failure and feelings allows the audience to understand the real story behind the on-screen moment, fostering a stronger connection with his community.

## Notable Moments
- Linus describing his state of mind during the failure: "I was full deer in headlights, guys. I was mortified."
- After the failed demo, Linus was so hyper-focused on troubleshooting that he "booked it" off stage, sprinting past the host's desk and ignoring the usual post-segment pleasantries, a moment he found hilarious in hindsight.
- On resurrecting old GPUs: "As long as we can get enough wafers, then I actually love this because and you can hate Nvidia for a lot of reasons... But one of the things that you can't really hate them for... is supporting their products for a long time."
- Discussing Tesla's move to make Full Self-Driving a subscription, Linus's colleague quipped in the show notes: "Full self-driving doesn't even work as promised, and he's already taking away the ability to purchase it."
