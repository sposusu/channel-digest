# 開箱 Digital Storm 的迷你高效能電腦工作站

## 重點摘要
Digital Storm 打造了一台極其小巧（僅 15 公升多）的電競電腦/工作站，卻蘊含著驚人效能。它採用巧妙的散熱設計，讓 RTX Pro 6000 等高階組件能在小巧的機身內高效運行。

## 故事大綱
- **開場：** 影片開頭，主持人展示了一台 Digital Storm 的超小型電競電腦/工作站，並對其內部蘊藏的強大性能感到驚訝。
- **中段：** 在一次搞笑的失誤後，主持人說明這是一個非常早期的樣品並打開機箱。他強調其核心設計理念是最大化「每單位體積的效能」。影片揭示了內部裝有一張強大的 RTX Pro 6000 顯示卡，並解說了機殼獨特的側進側出風流散熱系統，同時應用於 GPU 和 CPU 水冷排。
- **結尾：** 主持人再次強調這台 15 公升機殼所裝載的驚人動力，並詢問觀眾會想把這樣一台便攜又強大的機器帶到哪裡去。

## 關鍵見解
- **效能密度：** Digital Storm 設計這台電腦的主要重點是「效能密度」（performance per volume），在最小的體積內發揮最大效能。
- **極致小巧：** 機殼體積僅 15 公升多，對於一台能容納專業級硬體的工作站來說極其小巧。
- **高階硬體：** 它能夠容納像 RTX Pro 6000 這樣的高階專業 GPU，顯示其強大的擴充潛力。
- **創新散熱：** 機殼採用高效的「貫穿式」散熱設計，讓關鍵組件能直接吸入外部冷空氣並快速排出熱氣，解決了小機殼常見的散熱難題。
- **混合定位：** 該產品的定位同時是頂級電競電腦和專業工作站，滿足不同使用者的需求。

## 精彩時刻
- 主持人初見電腦時的讚嘆：「Wow」。
- 兩次打破嚴肅氣氛的搞笑時刻：「Damn it」。
- 在展示內部強大的 RTX Pro 6000 顯示卡時，興奮地喊出：「Woo!」。

# A Look Inside Digital Storm's Tiny, High-Performance PC

## TL;DR
Digital Storm has created an incredibly compact (just over 15L) gaming PC/workstation that packs a serious punch. It features a clever cooling design that allows high-end components like an RTX Pro 6000 to run efficiently in a small form factor.

## Story Flow
- **Beginning:** The video starts with the presenter showing off a very small gaming PC/workstation from Digital Storm, expressing amazement at how much power is inside.
- **Middle:** After a comedic "damn it," the presenter reveals the product is an early sample and dives inside. They highlight the core design philosophy—maximizing performance per volume—and unveil a powerful RTX Pro 6000 GPU, explaining the case's unique side-to-side airflow cooling system.
- **End:** The presenter reiterates the impressive power packed into the small 15L chassis and asks the audience where they would take such a portable and powerful machine.

## Key Insights
- **Performance Density:** A major focus for this Digital Storm PC is achieving maximum performance per unit of volume.
- **Extremely Compact:** The chassis is just over 15 liters, making it exceptionally small for a workstation capable of housing professional-grade hardware.
- **High-End Components:** It can fit powerful professional GPUs like the RTX Pro 6000, showcasing its high-performance potential.
- **Innovative Cooling:** The case features a highly efficient "flow-through" cooling design, giving key components direct access to fresh air and allowing for quick heat exhaustion.
- **Hybrid Positioning:** The product is positioned as both a high-end gaming PC and a professional workstation.

## Notable Moments
- The presenter's initial "Wow" upon seeing the compact PC.
- The two comedic "Damn it" interjections that break the serious tone.
- The excited "Woo!" when revealing the powerful RTX Pro 6000 graphics card inside.
